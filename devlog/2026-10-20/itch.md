Title: Devlog #2: the Factory, the Warden, and permadeath (05.10-20.10)

It has been two and a half weeks since the last devlog, and a lot has changed. Thank you for playing and for all your notes!

📢 came from your feedback. Everything else came from watching how you play.

## 🏭 The Factory ⚙️

Added a factory of the cult. Floors five and six are now THE FOUNDRY and THE ENGINE, with iron walls, machines everywhere and new enemies made of metal.

- Moving traps: presses, conveyor belts, burners and rams. They always warn you before they hit, and you can lure the cult into them (much more fun).
- Six new iron enemies: the Sawbot, the Welder, the Arm, the Sweeper, the Cartridge and the Shield Generator. Fire and poison mostly don't work on them, so you need to find another way.
- 🤖 **The Automaton** waits at the end, a huge walking machine. You can't reach its body, but its legs are a different story.

## 🍷 The Warden

Finally the man who took your wife has a face.

- **The Warden** shows up three times before you can fight him, so watch for him.
- **The Last Supper.** The last floor ends at his table, and he has an offer for you.
- He also has a second form. That is all we will say :)

## Enemies and combat

- **The Shaman.** Gives his spirit to the clubmen and makes them stronger, and can call you to him. Better kill him first.
- **The Coward.** A cultist without his mask who hides in a corner and begs for his life. What to do with him is your choice.
- Every floor's last boss got his own short scene and a boss bar.
- Three horn types: SHORT, BIG and LONG, and each of them plays very differently.
- 📢 Stealth is in the game from the second floor (press ALT).

## Rooms

- **Snow** on THE ROAD. You leave tracks in it.
- Rooms with a trench, where you can go straight at the enemies or roll over the gap and come from behind.
- New boss arenas.
- The Sacrifice Altar in THE CAVE. Everything that stands on it pays.

## Progression

- **Permadeath.** If you die, the run is over, but heaven and your unlocks stay.
- **Heaven is now the animals' home.** Bring them up there and take their dares.
- **Capes.** Every cape gives you one new move on Q (some of them are well hidden).
- Now you can wear up to three talismans.
- 5 new souls and 3 new talismans.
- Quitting to the menu doesn't count as a death anymore, CONTINUE puts you back where you were.

## The rest

- Animals talk now, each one in its own voice.
- 📢 You can rebind any action in Settings, mouse buttons too.
- A small map of the rooms you have already seen.
- Reworked animation: the goat and the cultists run on real legs now.
- The goat runs a bit faster.

## Fixes

- No more black flash on the screen on every headbutt.
- The goat doesn't get stuck in corners anymore.
