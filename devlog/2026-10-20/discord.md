**Devlog #2: the Factory, the Warden, and permadeath** (05.10-20.10)

It has been two and a half weeks since the last devlog, and a lot has changed. Thank you for playing and for all your notes!

📢 came from your feedback.

**🏭 The Factory ⚙️**
Added a factory of the cult. Floors five and six are now THE FOUNDRY and THE ENGINE, with moving traps and six new enemies made of iron (fire and poison mostly don't work on them). 🤖 At the end waits a huge walking Automaton, and its legs are in reach.

**🍷 The Warden**
Finally the man who took your wife has a face. You see him three times before the fight, and the last floor ends at his table, where he has an offer for you.

**Enemies and combat**
- **The Shaman** makes clubmen stronger and can call you to him.
- **The Coward** hides in a corner and begs. Your choice what to do with him.
- Every last boss got a scene and a boss bar.
- Three horn types: SHORT, BIG, LONG.
- 📢 Stealth from the second floor (press ALT).

**Rooms**
- Snow on THE ROAD, you leave tracks in it.
- Trench rooms, new boss arenas, the Sacrifice Altar in THE CAVE.

**Progression**
- **Permadeath.** Heaven and unlocks stay.
- Heaven is now the animals' home, take their dares.
- **Capes** give a new move on Q. Up to three talismans now.
- 5 new souls, 3 new talismans.
- Quit to the menu and CONTINUE where you were.

**The rest**
- Animals talk now.
- 📢 Rebind any action. A small map of the rooms.
- Reworked animation, real legs for the goat and the cultists. The goat runs a bit faster.
- Fixes: no black flash on headbutt, no getting stuck in corners.

See you upstairs.
