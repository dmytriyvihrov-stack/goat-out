Title: Devlog #1: heaven, a guy who throws goats, and a fat Spartan

Big week. The second wave of closed playtests, with more than 5 hours of gameplay. Over 100 improvements, big and small.

Thank you for taking part!

In more detail, here's what changed and got better this week (builds 1.78 to 1.99, all live now).

📢 Lines marked like this came straight from you. Everything else came from watching you play.

## ☁️ Heaven 😇

Before, a death just sent you back to the start of the floor. Now you go somewhere first.

- When the goat dies, he goes up to heaven. It's a quiet field above the clouds.
- Someone is waiting for you there. He has a job for you.
- 👻 The souls of the cultists you kill don't just disappear anymore.
- 🪞 There is a broken mirror. Maybe it can be fixed.
- When you are ready, jump off the edge.

## Enemies and combat

Two new enemies:

- **The Thrower.** One of his arms is huge. The other is tiny. He picks up things and throws them at you: crates, bombs, his friends, even your animals. Get too close and he throws you too.
- **The Shieldman.** A fat Spartan with a shield made of skulls. Don't headbutt the shield. It hurts you. Go around him.

Old enemies, new tricks:

- The Butcher has a hook now. Watch out for it.
- 📢 The Ogre does not like headbutts. You bounce right off him.
- Don't keep the Butcher stuck in a corner. After a few hits he pushes you away.

Easier to read in a fight:

- The mouse pointer is now the headbutt icon. Now it's clear what the left click does.
- Every kill has its own sound. You hear it over the music.
- When you burn, a flame grows on the goat. When it is full, you lose a heart. So you always know how much time you have.
- Swords can get stuck in big enemies. They stay there until the enemy dies.
- Broken swords and shields fall apart into pieces. They don't just disappear.
- Explosions don't go through walls and doors anymore.
- 📢 The stone teeth in the cave look dangerous now.

## Rooms

New things in rooms you can use:

- **Chandeliers.** Cut the rope at the wall. The chandelier falls on whoever is below.
- 📢 **Suits of armour.** Knock one down and it falls apart. Or take its halberd and throw it.
- **Stag heads** on the walls. Throw a cultist into the antlers and he stays there.
- Push a table hard and it falls on its side.
- Red barrels blow each other up. Green barrels spill poison.
- Spilled gunpowder explodes when fire touches it.

New rooms: the Armory, three new mixed rooms and three more for THE DARK. 📢 THE BRIDGE now has a real bridge.

## Progression

- **Keys.** Bosses sometimes drop a key. Some things are locked in iron cages. You won't have keys for everything. Choose well.
- The stairs at the end of each floor are locked now. The last boss has the key, in a way.
- Each soul gives you two cards to choose from, not one. Sometimes you get a third. If a slot is full, you can swap.
- Fire souls work better together. So do poison souls.
- New souls: Bull Neck, Spring Hocks, Big Lungs and Ricochet.
- New talismans: The Magnet, The Nosebag and The Knucklebone.

## Animals

- The horse wants to race you to the soul rooms.
- The pig is hungry.
- New animals: a rabbit and a husky.
- Every animal tells you what it wants. You can say yes or no.

## Controls and UI

- You can play with a gamepad now.
- There is a keyboard-only mode too: J K L (or Z X C).
- Photo mode. Turn it on in Settings and save the moments you like.
- Between floors a small map shows how far you got.
- Press I to open the Book. It shows all your souls, grouped by the part of the goat they change.

## Look and sound

- A new title screen.
- The music has a longer tune. Fights sound bigger. Quiet moments are quieter.

## Balance

- 📢 All four fire souls together made you fireproof. That was too strong. Now they don't.
- The roll takes you a bit further. It also takes a bit longer to use again.
- After a headbutt you need a bit more time before the next one. So every headbutt counts.
- Hounds dodge less often.

## Fixes

- The small freeze every few seconds is mostly gone.
- 📢 On itch, the keyboard sometimes stopped working after a click outside the game. Fixed.
- 📢 The camera doesn't jump between rooms anymore. It also stopped shaking on 16:10 screens.
- A soul from a gate now always lands on your side of the gate.
- 📢 Hounds have learned that holes are not floor.
- 📢 The food on tables looked like something to pick up. Now it looks less tasty.
- Small text is bigger now, on phones too.

Thank you for playing and for all your notes. See you upstairs.
