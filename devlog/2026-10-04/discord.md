**Devlog #1** · 28 Sep - 4 Oct · builds 1.78-1.99, all live

Big week. The second wave of closed playtests, with more than 5 hours of gameplay. Over 100 improvements, big and small.
Thank you for taking part!

📢 came straight from you. The rest came from watching you play.

**☁️ Heaven 😇**
Before, a death sent you back to the start. Now the goat goes to heaven first.
Someone is waiting for you there. He has a job for you.
👻 The souls of the cultists you kill don't just disappear anymore. 🪞 And there is a broken mirror.

**Enemies and combat**
- The Thrower: one huge arm. He throws crates, bombs, his friends, your animals. And goats
- The Shieldman: a fat Spartan with a skull shield. Don't headbutt it
- The Butcher has a hook now. 📢 You bounce off the Ogre
- Easier to read: headbutt pointer, a sound for every kill, a flame on the goat shows how long you have

**Rooms**
Chandeliers to drop. 📢 Suits of armour to knock down. Stag heads to hang cultists on. Tables that fall over. Barrels that blow each other up. Plus the Armory and six new rooms

**Progression**
Keys and iron cages (you won't have keys for everything). Two soul cards, sometimes three. Fire and poison sets. Four new souls, three new talismans

**Animals**
A horse that wants to race, a hungry pig, a rabbit and a husky. They all tell you what they want

**Also**
Gamepad, keyboard-only mode, photo mode, a map between floors, the Book on I, a longer tune

**Fixes**
The small freeze is mostly gone. 📢 The keyboard on itch works again. 📢 The camera stopped jumping. 📢 Hounds have learned that holes are not floor
