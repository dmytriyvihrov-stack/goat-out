- (21 Oct) **Three zones, three questions** (his idea, "keep as references and ideas, don't change the structure for now"; for the Godot port, never the frozen JS). Roughly three floors a zone. One reference holds the whole arc: **Dusk**'s three episodes (E1 The Foothills: farms and cultists; E2 The Industrial Revolution: factories; E3 The Nameless City: the place itself is wrong).
  - *Zone 1, the cult (flesh)*: "can you fight?" The wall kills, straight through works. The verbs are taught here: move, fight, roll. Reference: Dusk E1.
  - *Zone 2, the machines (iron)*: "do you know how to break this?" Immunities; the wall does not kill iron, its own works do (the press, the fall, the blast, the short). Find the way, not the longer fight. Reference: Quake II's Strogg (not Quake 1, which is gothic and belongs to zone 3), Dusk E2.
  - *Zone 3, the mirage (shadow)*: "do you trust what you see?" Pitch: **Here the space lies. Every lie leaves a trace.** The dark, wraiths, mimics (crate, door, stool), THE TRIP. Rules: the space lies, a blow never does (an honest windup, pillar 4); every lie has a tell for the attentive (planks breathing, tracks in snow with nobody in them, a wrong shadow); where a mimic stands is rolled anew (pillar 6). Look: desaturated, one accent, violet (witchfire, corrupted souls). References: Dusk E3, Darkwood (top-down, sight, things in the dark), Faith: The Unholy Trinity (pixel horror on a bare palette), Prey 2017 (mimics), World of Horror, Quake 1.
  - A rough fit, not decided: zone 1 ALTAR, YARD, ROAD; zone 2 FOUNDRY, ENGINE and one more machine floor; zone 3 CAVE/THE DARK, RAFTERS, OSSUARY, with THE TRIP in it and snow a modifier outside the zones. Each zone keeps the last in its mix and ends on a boss that examines it (BANE, the automaton, the Warden). Open: does THE DARK stay a secret branch or become a zone 3 floor?
- (21 Oct) **Enemy ideas, "possible, for later"** (brainstorm, none chosen; a common thread: the cult treats the goat as livestock). Starred are the three I'd take first.
  - ★ *THE MOUNTED* (his idea, zone 3 or 1): a stuffed beast that looks like a wall trophy (its glass eyes follow him), crawls along the wall's face round the room and never leaves it; lunges out 1-2 tiles and sticks out a beat. Grabbed while out, it is torn from the wall and is a flopping straw sack on the floor: then any wall or fire. The wall is its home, not its death.
  - *THE DROVER* (zone 1): a crook hooks him by the neck and leads him like cattle toward a pen or a lip; roll or BAAH frees him; he deals no harm himself.
  - *THE BRANDER* (zone 1): a hot brand, slow windup; branded, the whole room knows where he is for N s, no stealth. Glows in the dark.
  - ★ *THE GLUTTON* (zone 1): walks to bodies and eats them, +1 heart and bigger each; a corpse is bait, thrown to a press or a drop he follows it.
  - *THE BLOATED* (zone 1-2): bursts on death into a pool of blood; bodies slide further on it, so the wall kills from further.
  - *Carcasses on hooks* (zone 1-2, a room's furniture): butted, a carcass swings and knocks men into walls; in zone 3 one is alive.
  - *THE STITCHED* (zone 3): two men sewn back to back, no back to come round to; killed, he tears into two crawling halves.
  - *THE GRUBS* (zone 3): worms out of the bodies in dark rooms, one heart each, cling and slow him, the roll shakes them off; a room grows worse the longer bodies lie.
  - *THE SOW* (zone 1, a mini boss): huge, in a nest, births piglet-cultists; target number one; woken, she rams.
  - *THE SHEARER* (zone 1-2): shears that close from both sides: right in front is safe, the flanks are not.
  - ★ *THE MIRROR GOAT* (zone 3): a cultist in a black goat's skin who mirrors his verbs; killed only by standing so his mirrored butt goes into stone or teeth. Answers the open "mirrors" half of "lizards and mirrors".
  - *THE TONGUE* (zone 3): out of a floor drain, pulls him to the hole; the drain can't be hurt, a crate or a body thrown in chokes it.
  - *A shadow with nobody* (zone 3): a man's shadow on the floor, the man unseen, given away by the shadow and his tracks.
  - *THE KILL LINE* (zone 2, a room): carcasses riding a belt to a saw; a man thrown on it goes the same way (THE FEED in meat).
  - *THE GRAFTED* (zone 2): half a man, half a machine; fire takes only the flesh side.
