Goat Out: every sound effect

Each press plays a random take of the effect through the game's own mix and its small dry room (js/foley.js, js/audio.js). Raise Music to hear the room score's bed and pick its theme and stage under Music. Press twice: no two takes are the same. Serve the project (node tools/serve.js 8766) and open /tools/sfx-board.html.
